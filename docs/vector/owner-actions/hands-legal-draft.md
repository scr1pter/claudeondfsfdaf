# Vector Hands legal copy proposals

Draft for owner/legal review only. This file does not modify the live privacy policy or terms. Confirm section numbering against the current pages before approving a change.

## Privacy policy, section 6

Proposed addition:

> When you enable Vector Hands, Vector may capture the screen or read accessibility information from apps you approve to complete your request. This content can include personal information in those apps. Screenshots and conversation history are stored on your computer and content needed for the request is sent to the model provider you selected. That provider's terms and data handling apply. Your screenshot-retention choice and any company maximum govern local retention. Deleting local screenshots does not recall content already sent to a provider or shared with another person. Conversation shares exclude screenshots unless you explicitly include them. App approvals do not authorize access to protected credential fields or operating-system consent prompts.

## Terms, section 5

Proposed addition:

> Vector Hands operates approved desktop apps while you remain available to supervise and answer questions. Review sensitive-action requests and shared screenshots before approving them. You may stop a run or take over at any time. Do not use the feature to bypass permissions, administer a protected desktop, access another person's accounts or operate a computer without authorization. Availability depends on the operating system, app accessibility support and permissions. Locked-session operation is not included.

Review these proposals against the shipped implementation, provider contracts and applicable retention obligations. Approval of release notes does not automatically approve legal changes.
