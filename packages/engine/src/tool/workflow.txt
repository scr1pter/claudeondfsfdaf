Run a bounded workflow of subagents from JSON, or a trusted saved workflow name. Use action:"guide" for the grammar and three examples, then action:"validate" to review its plan without starting agents. action:"run" (default) starts approved work in the background; action:"resume" requires run_id and can accept an edited spec or inputs. retry_failed defaults to true. Model-written specs are limited to 32 KiB and each input to 8 KiB. Use files, changed_files or from instead of large literal lists. Stages can fan out, depend on results and verify work; budgets, permissions and agent/model policies still apply. Workflows start from the main agent, require background subagents, and are unavailable for guests or Light/capped requests. Stop and inspect runs through task_manage. Partial results survive limits and interruption; restart never resumes automatically. Workflows describe fixed data flow, never executable code.
